# The Station
<img width="1022" height="281" alt="readmebanner" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ffd9f424-a2f4-49dc-8a84-2d2f67d62a26" />


Created for both The Singapore Police Force's Delta Challenge, as well as Ngee Ann Polytechnic's Year 2 Integrated Studio Project, by 33.3% Apple.

The Station is a game that aims to educate the general public on the general warning signs of Crime. The game is set in a Singapore MRT Station, with its gameplay heavily inspired by Exit 8.

Catch bad actors and feel the difference between action and inaction! (2 Different Endings can be achieved)

## Walkthrough/Guide

To win the game, you have to complete 10 loops. If you pick the wrong option, the loop will reset.

You should keep moving forward.
Upon seeing a normal anomaly, you should turn around.
<br>
**These anomalies include:**
- Posters scattered across the room
- Misplaced travellator 
- Broken fire extinguisher 
- A man standing still and runs towards you if you are near

<br>

There are also story-based anomalies that will impact the ending should you choose to interact or ignore. <br>
**There are a total of 5 story-based anomalies to encounter that are in chronological order:**
- A guy standing peeking over at a woman 
- The girl is on the travellator and the guy is watching 
- The girl moves travellators and he follows after a short delay 
- The guy is standing closer to her 
- The guy takes a photo of her

If you interact with all 5, you achieve the 'Good Ending', where you are able to exit the station!<br>
However, not interacting with all 5 will get you the 'Bad Ending'.
